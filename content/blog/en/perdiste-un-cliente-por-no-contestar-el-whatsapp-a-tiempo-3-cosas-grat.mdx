## You Lost a Client for Not Answering WhatsApp on Time: 3 Free Things You Can Do Today

It's 8:47 PM on Tuesday. You open WhatsApp Business and see 14 unanswered messages. Three are from this morning. One arrived Saturday at 3 PM and says "Hi, I want to know if you have availability for tomorrow." It's already Tuesday. That person already booked with someone else.

60% of customers define "immediate" as 10 minutes or less [1]. 78% of customers buy from the first business that responds [2]. If you take more than an hour, it doesn't matter if your service is better: you already lost.

At Impulsa Lab we run AI automation for SMBs, but this article isn't about paid chatbots. It's about the three free tools that WhatsApp Business already has inside your phone and that most owners never configure.

## What Changed in 2026

88% of customers say they expect faster responses than a year ago [3]. Expectations don't adjust to the size of your business. Customers don't differentiate between large companies and small businesses when it comes to response time: they expect the same speed, the same availability, and the same channel flexibility, regardless of the size of your team [1].

- 90% of consumers rate an "immediate" response as important when they have a customer service question, and 60% define "immediate" as 10 minutes or less [4].
- Responding in the first hour increases conversion rates between 400% and 500% [5].
- Leads contacted in 5 minutes are 21 times more likely to qualify [6].
- 52% of customers say they will stop buying from a company after a slow support experience. More than 50% will switch to a competitor after a bad experience involving slow response [3].

The problem isn't that you don't have people. It's that when the customer writes at 7 PM on Sunday, there's no one on the other side. And when Monday arrives at 9 AM and you see that message, it's already too late.

## What This Looks Like in Practice: The 3 Free WhatsApp Business Tools

WhatsApp Business App is free and has three automatic reply functions that don't require programming anything. In the WhatsApp Business App, sending automatic replies is free [7]. Your phone only needs to be on and connected to internet for them to work.

### 1. Away Message

It's a native function of the WhatsApp Business app that Meta makes available to any business account for free [8]. Its purpose is simple but critical: inform the customer that you received their message and that you'll respond as soon as possible [8].

Setup (takes 2 minutes):
- Open WhatsApp Business
- Tap the three dots (Android) or go to Settings (iOS)
- Select Business tools
- Tap Away message
- Activate "Send away message"
- Write your message

Example of effective message:
"Thanks for writing. We received your message outside our hours (Mon-Fri 9AM-6PM). We'll respond tomorrow before noon. If it's urgent: [emergency number or alternative]."

Then tap "Schedule" to choose when you want this message to be sent automatically. You have three options: Always send (sends the message every time someone contacts you), Custom schedule (you can set specific hours), Outside business hours (sends the message only during the hours you designated as outside your business hours in the app settings) [9].

The most practical setting for an SMB: "Outside business hours". That way the message only sends when you're really unavailable, not every time someone writes you.

In the standard app, your phone must be online for the automatic reply to go out. With the API solution (cloud-based), it works even when your phone is off [10]. But that's already a paid solution, not what this article covers.

### 2. Greeting Message

WhatsApp's greeting message is automatically triggered in two scenarios: at first contact and after 14 days of inactivity [11]. It's not the same as an away message [11].

This message is sent when someone writes you for the first time. It's your opportunity to say who you are, what you do and what they can expect.

Setup:
- WhatsApp Business → Business tools → Greeting message
- Activate "Send greeting message"
- Write the message

Example for a mechanic shop:
"Hi! Thanks for contacting [Shop Name]. We do diagnostics, brakes, suspension and general service. Hours: Mon-Sat 8AM-5PM. How can we help you today?"

Example for a hair salon:
"Hi, welcome to [Name]. We do cut, color, straightening and treatments. To book an appointment send us your name, service and preferred day/time. We respond in less than 1 hour during 10AM-7PM hours."

The difference between greeting and away: greeting is sent only the first time someone contacts you or after 14 days without talking. Away is sent every time someone writes outside your hours. You can (and should) activate both.

### 3. Quick Replies

In the WhatsApp Business app, you can save up to 50 quick replies. Each shortcut can have up to 25 characters, and each message up to 500 characters [12].

Quick replies are NOT automatic. Quick replies still require a human to select and send each one. They speed up response time, but don't eliminate the agent from the process [13]. But they save you from writing the same thing 40 times per week.

Setup:
- WhatsApp Business → Business tools → Quick replies
- Tap "Add"
- Write the message
- Assign a shortcut (must start with "/")
- Save

Examples of useful shortcuts:

**Shortcut:** /hours  
**Message:** "Our hours are Mon-Fri 9AM-6PM, Sat 10AM-3PM. Closed Sundays."

**Shortcut:** /prices  
**Message:** "Men's cut is $25, women's $40, kids $18. Straightening from $120 depending on hair length."

**Shortcut:** /address  
**Message:** "We're at [full address]. Google Maps link: [link]. There's street parking."

**Shortcut:** /thanks  
**Message:** "Thanks for contacting us. If you have more questions write us, we respond in less than 1 hour during office hours."

When a customer writes you "how much is a cut", instead of typing everything again, you open the chat, type "/prices" and the message auto-completes. You review it, you send it. 5 seconds instead of 45.

Typing "/" in the message box shows your saved replies, and selecting one sends it instantly without having to type again [13].

## When NOT to Use Only These Free Automatic Messages

These three functions are the floor, not the ceiling. They work if you meet these conditions:

1. **You receive less than 50 messages per week.** If you receive 200, quick replies don't scale. You need a real automated flow or more people responding.

2. **The questions you receive are 80% repetitive.** Hours, prices, location, availability. If each conversation requires unique context (like a lawyer reviewing a case), quick replies don't solve anything.

3. **You have fixed hours and someone can respond during those hours.** If you work alone and go out to the field 6 hours a day, the away message covers you outside hours, but you still lose customers who write at 11 AM when you're on a job. There you need an AI agent or to hire someone.

If you DON'T meet all three, free isn't enough. You need WhatsApp Business API with automated flows or an AI agent. That already has cost: between 297 USD/month and 497 USD/month for an SMB, depending on conversation volume and flow complexity.

## The Most Common Error We See

Most owners configure the away message once, with generic text like "We are not available at this time", and never update it.

That message doesn't work. The customer doesn't know when you're going to respond. They don't know if their message reached a closed business or someone busy. They have no alternative.

An effective away message includes three things:

1. **Confirmation that you received the message.**  
   "Thanks for writing. We received your message."

2. **When you're going to respond.**  
   "We respond to all messages in less than 2 hours during Mon-Fri 9AM-6PM hours. It's now [time outside hours], we'll respond tomorrow starting at 9 AM."

3. **Alternative if it's urgent.**  
   "If it's an emergency, call [number] or write to [email/other channel]."

Without those three elements, the away message generates more frustration than relief.

Configure this today. It takes 10 minutes for the three tools. If tomorrow at 8 PM someone writes you asking if you have availability for the weekend, they're going to receive an automatic message instead of silence. And Monday, when you respond, that person is still waiting for your response instead of having bought from someone else on Sunday.
