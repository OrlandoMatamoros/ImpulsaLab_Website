## The 3 Free Things You Can Configure Today (With Exact Steps)

### 1. Automatic Welcome Message

WhatsApp Business includes two types of native automatic messages: the welcome message (sent on first contact or after 14 days without interaction) and the away message (activated outside business hours), both are free and configurable from the app settings.

**What it does:** Sends automatically the first time someone writes you or if the conversation has been inactive for more than 14 days.

**What it's for:** Confirms you received the message, gives context about your business, and—this is key—answers the most frequent questions before they ask them.

**How to configure it (4 minutes):**

1. Open WhatsApp Business
2. Tap the three dots top right → Business tools → Welcome message
3. Activate the toggle
4. Write your message. Example for a nail salon:

> Hi, I'm María from Bella Nails Salon 💅  
> Hours: Mon-Sat 10 AM-8 PM  
> Classic manicure: 25 USD | Gel: 45 USD | Full acrylic: 60 USD  
> How can I help you?

**What to always include:**
- Business name
- Hours
- 3-4 most requested prices/services (this eliminates 60% of repetitive questions we've seen in our active clients)
- Open question at the end

**What NOT to include:**
- Links (many clients don't open them)
- More than 6 lines (nobody reads long blocks on WhatsApp)
- Corporate language ("At our company we specialize in..." — sounds like bad bot)

### 2. Away Message for Outside Hours

The away message activates outside business hours, and it's the most underestimated tool in WhatsApp Business.

**What it does:** Sends automatically when someone writes outside your configured hours.

**What it's for:** Manages expectations. The client knows you saw their message, knows when you're going to respond, and—this reduces abandonment—doesn't feel you ignored them.

**How to configure it (4 minutes):**

1. Go to Business tools → Away message
2. Activate the toggle
3. Configure your availability hours (example: Mon-Fri 9 AM-6 PM, Sat 10 AM-4 PM)
4. Write your message. Example for a hardware store:

> Hi, we received your message outside hours.  
> We open Mon-Fri 9 AM-6 PM, Sat 10 AM-4 PM.  
> I'll respond as soon as we open. If urgent call 555-1234.

**The detail that changes everything:** Include an urgency option (phone, backup WhatsApp, or "if you need something today, the Home Depot on 5th street is open until 10 PM"). Sounds counterintuitive to recommend competition, but in our experience with hardware stores and bodegas, the client remembers that gesture and comes back to you for the next purchase.

**Common mistakes:**
- Not configuring different hours for each day (if you close Sundays, mark it)
- Putting "we'll respond soon" without saying when "soon" is
- Writing the message in third person ("the X team will respond") when it's just you (the client knows it and sounds fake)

### 3. Quick Replies for the 5 Questions They Always Ask You

The WhatsApp Business app allows you to configure predefined quick replies for the most common queries.

**What it does:** Saves pre-written responses that appear when you type "/" followed by a keyword.

**What it's for:** You answer in 4 seconds what normally takes you 45 seconds to write (hours, address, prices, cancellation policy, payment methods).

**How to configure it (3 minutes):**

1. Go to Business tools → Quick replies
2. Tap the "+" to add new
3. Write your shortcut (example: /hours)
4. Write the complete message

**The 5 quick replies that EVERY SMB needs:**

**1. /hours**
> We open Mon-Fri 9-6, Sat 10-4. Closed Sundays.

**2. /address**
> We're at 23rd Street #456, between 5th and 6th Ave, Queens NY 11372. [add Google Maps link if you want]

**3. /prices**
> Men's cut: 20 USD | Beard: 15 USD | Cut+beard combo: 30 USD | Kids: 15 USD

**4. /payment**
> We accept cash, Zelle, Venmo and all cards.

**5. /cancel**
> If you need to cancel or change your appointment, let me know at least 3 hours in advance so you don't lose your spot.

**How to use them:** When a client asks "what are your hours?", instead of typing the entire response, you type /hours, select the pre-saved response, and send. 4 seconds vs 45 seconds. If you handle 15 questions a day, that's 10 minutes saved daily, 50 minutes weekly.

## When NOT to Use Only These 3 Free Features

These settings work if you meet these 3 criteria:

1. **You receive less than 40 client messages per day.** If you receive more, you need a system that qualifies leads and routes conversations, not just automatic entry messages.

2. **You or someone on your team can respond manually within 2-4 hours during business hours.** If your actual response time is consistently greater than 6 hours, the expectations generated by these automatic messages (which promise quick response) will worsen the experience, not improve it.

3. **80% of the questions you receive have simple answers (price, hours, availability, location).** If your business requires technical consultations, personalized quotes, or conversations of more than 6 messages to close a sale, then you need a chatbot with conditional logic or an AI agent that understands context.

**Example of when it's NOT enough:** An insurance consultant receives 50 messages a day, each lead requires understanding family situation, comparing 3 policies, and scheduling a video call. In that case, welcome message + away help, but don't replace an automated workflow that qualifies the lead, asks discovery questions, and schedules the call without manual intervention.

## The Most Common Mistake We See (and How to Avoid It)

The owner configures the three messages, it works well the first week, and then deactivates them because "they sound too automatic" or "clients complain it's a robot."

The problem isn't the automatic messages. The problem is **how they're written**.

**Bad welcome message (sounds like generic bot):**
> Thank you for contacting [Company Name]. A member of our team will contact you as soon as possible. Service hours: M-F 9-18h.

**Good welcome message (sounds human, gives useful info):**
> Hi! I'm Carlos from JM Shop. I'll respond in a couple minutes. Meanwhile: we open M-F 9-6, we repair brakes from 120 USD and oil change 45 USD. What do you need?

The difference: real name, first person, concrete prices, direct question.

**Golden rule:** If your automatic message doesn't sound like you talking on WhatsApp with a friend asking about your business, rewrite it.

## What Comes After This (The Path to Real Automation)

The three settings in this article are the floor, not the ceiling. They save you from losing clients due to silence, but don't scale if your business grows.

**The natural progression we see in our clients is:**

**Stage 1 (today):** Welcome message + away + quick replies. Cost: 0 USD/month. Handles up to 40 messages/day if you respond manually during hours.

**Stage 2 (when you receive 40-100 messages/day):** Basic chatbot with conditional logic using tools like Typebot or Manychat. Cost: 15-50 USD/month. Automatically qualifies leads ("Looking for cut, beard or both?" → routes to specific responses), schedules appointments, sends reminders.

**Stage 3 (when you receive 100+ messages/day or your average ticket exceeds 300 USD):** AI agent connected to WhatsApp Business API that understands natural language, checks your inventory in real time, quotes, and escalates complex cases to human only when necessary. Cost: 150-400 USD/month depending on volume. Requires Business Solution Provider (BSP) for API access.

At Impulsa Lab we run Stage 3 for clients billing above 500K USD/year who have sales teams. But most SMBs we talk to in discovery are at Stage 1 or haven't configured it yet. If that's your case, start with the three free features in this article **before** thinking about paying for automation.

**For WhatsApp Business API access (Stage 3):**
- The WhatsApp Business app is free, but the WhatsApp Business API is designed for growing teams that need automation, integrations and messaging at scale; API access itself is free, but you pay for the messages you send according to categories, volume and region
- Hyperleap plans start at 40 USD/month for the Plus plan, with a 7-day free trial
- In the official Cloud API, each incoming message from a buyer opens a free 24-hour response window; outside that window, template messages have a per-message fee

**Our internal stack at Impulsa Lab for enterprise clients:**
- n8n (self-hosted) for workflows between WhatsApp, CRM (HubSpot or Pipedrive), and knowledge base
- Claude Sonnet 4 via Anthropic API for natural language processing (0.003 USD per 1K input tokens, 0.015 USD per 1K output tokens)

**We don't recommend** (although you'll see them in searches): Solutions that charge per "message sent" instead of per conversation (inflate costs 3-5x), platforms that aren't official Meta BSPs (risk of number ban), and any tool that promises "free AI forever" (eventually they migrate you to paid plan when you depend on them).
