## When NOT to Use Claude for Small Business

We've seen the mistake in clients who arrive excited after reading Anthropic's announcement. Claude for Small Business is not the answer if:

**1. Your stack is not QuickBooks + HubSpot + Google/Microsoft**. If you run NetSuite, Pipedrive, Zoho or any vertical ERP (ServiceTitan for HVAC, Toast for restaurants), the native connectors don't apply. You can build custom connectors via n8n or Make, but the out-of-the-box value is lost. In those cases, stick with linear workflows in Zapier until Anthropic announces more partners or hire an operator to build the connectors (average quote: 1,200-2,400 USD one-time).

**2. Your transaction volume is less than 30 monthly**. If you process 8 invoices per month, chasing 2 overdue and closing the month takes you 40 minutes. Claude will save you 22 minutes. The cost of Claude Pro (20 USD/month) vs. that savings doesn't close. Breakeven is around 50-60 monthly transactions or 15+ hours of repetitive administrative work.

**3. You don't have a documented process for what you want to automate**. If your month-end close is "I look for things that seem weird and tell my accountant when something looks wrong", Claude won't guess what seems weird to you. Agentic workflows need structured input. Example: "flag discrepancies greater than 50 USD between QuickBooks and PayPal" works. "Check that everything is okay" doesn't work.

## The Most Common Mistake: Activating All Workflows the Same Day

The day Anthropic announced Claude for Small Business, we received 11 client inquiries asking if they should activate all 15 workflows at once. The answer is no. We've seen teams activate all 15 workflows in the first week and 10 days later disable 11 because they generate noise, duplicate approvals and outputs the team doesn't know how to use.

The rollout we recommend at Impulsa Lab:

**Week 1: One read-only workflow**. Start with "Business Pulse" or "Lead Triage", which read data from QuickBooks/HubSpot and send you a summary in Slack every morning. Zero write permissions. If the output is useful, leave it running. If not, adjust parameters before adding the next one.

**Week 2-3: One write workflow with manual approval**. "Invoice Chaser" or "Payroll Planning". Claude does the work and asks for your approval before anything gets sent, published or paid. Review each output for 2 weeks until you trust the quality. Then reduce review frequency.

**Week 4: Second write workflow**. "Month-End Close" or "Campaign Planner". Same pattern: manual approval the first 3-4 runs, then approval by exception (only review when Claude flags something out of range).

**Month 2 onward: Add workflows only when the pain is clear**. Don't activate "Contract Review" if you sign 2 contracts a year. Don't activate "Tax Season Prep" if your accountant already has a process that works. Workflows justify their cost only when they save 90+ minutes monthly or eliminate a recurring error that costs you money.

Three conditions for a Claude workflow to be worth it in your SMB:

1. **Repetitive**: you do it at least 2 times per month with the same steps.
2. **Structured**: you can write the 5-7 steps in a list without ambiguity.
3. **Cross-app**: it touches at least 2 tools (QuickBooks + PayPal, HubSpot + Canva, Gmail + QuickBooks). If everything happens within a single app, the workflow probably already exists as a native feature.

If your task meets all 3, activate the Claude workflow. If not, wait.
