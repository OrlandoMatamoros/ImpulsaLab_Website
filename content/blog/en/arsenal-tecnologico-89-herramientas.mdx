After two years testing tools for Latin SMBs in the US, we ended up with a library of 139 references. In early 2026 we ran a pruning exercise — we cut everything we had not used in the last 6 months, anything overrated, and anything with a clearly superior alternative. **89 tools** were left across 15 categories.

This post is the executive view of that arsenal. The full clickable version with ratings lives at [tuimpulsalab.com/herramientas/arsenal](/herramientas/arsenal).

## Why this list vs any "top 500 tools" list

A lot of "top AI tools" articles are affiliate farms. Ours pass three filters:

1. **We use them in production** — not in demo, not in "tried it once". Production.
2. **SMB-friendly budget** — most offer a free tier or a plan under USD 50/month.
3. **They survive an incident** — when something breaks on a Saturday at 11 PM, support or docs respond.

If a tool fails any of the three, out.

## What is NOT on the list (and why)

There are popular tools we cut:

- **Asana** — works, but ClickUp delivers more for the same price
- **Monday.com** — expensive for SMBs; ClickUp or Notion replace it
- **Slack Free** — if you do not pay, history caps at 90 days. For small teams WhatsApp + email is enough
- **Jasper** — Claude and ChatGPT replaced it without debate
- **Copy.ai** — same case as Jasper

Cutting them is not because they are bad. It is because **every extra tool is one more password, one more invoice, and one more failure point**. Less is more.

## How to pick yours

Our rule for clients: **start with 5, max 8 tools**. Add a new one only when one stopped working or when you have a concrete use case that justifies it. The most common mistake we see is SMBs paying for 9 SaaS subscriptions and actively using 3.
